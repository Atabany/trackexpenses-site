Support, from the developer.

Questions, bugs or corrections: atabany.apps@gmail.com. Include your app version, device and iOS version. Redact identifying details in samples.

Most questions are answered in the FAQ.

What stays free?

Unlimited manual transactions and pasted bank alerts, budgets, current-period budget moves and Cover, days left, budget alerts, bill reminders, historical stats, search with its totals and private iCloud sync across iPhone, iPad and Mac; up to 3 accounts and 2 recurring rules. Includes 10 successful automatic bank SMS or email imports, 10 saved voice entries and 10 AI questions to the money coach per calendar month on each device.

After the SMS allowance, later alerts are still caught and kept, but appear locked outside your totals; tapping one offers Pro. Upgrading adds them all to your ledger automatically. Otherwise, the next calendar month’s allowance releases as many as it covers. Nothing is dropped, and saved history is never locked.

What does Pro add?

Unlimited SMS and email imports, accounts and recurring rules; unlimited voice entry on compatible devices/languages; money coach challenges and many more coach questions; a salary plan, spending forecasts, overall and per-budget daily guidance, a daily budget for chosen categories and what-if planning; Export & Share (CSV of any range and the full JSON backup), CSV import, backup restore, themes and alternate icons. Monthly and annual subscriptions and a one-time lifetime purchase are available. Check current purchase details in the app.

If Pro or a trial ends, nothing you saved is locked: entries, accounts and repeating bills keep working, and a Pro theme and app icon come back if you get Pro again.

Restore or cancel

Tap Restore purchases in More (under Help & about), or Restore at the foot of the Pro screen, using the same Apple Account that bought Pro. If it remains locked, email the developer. Manage auto-renewing plans in Apple's subscription settings. Lifetime does not renew.

Your month and your records

More → Period & Entry changes your fixed monthly start day. Every export is part of Pro since version 1.15.0: the share button on Transactions, or More → Backup & Export, opens Export & Share, with CSV for this month or a longer span and a full JSON backup. Backup & Export also imports CSV and restores a backup. Your records stay in the app, free, and are never locked.

Move money and Cover

Open Stats → Budget for the current period in your budget’s own period length. Tap ••• on a budget, then Move money in or Move money out. Moves are free, apply to this period only and keep the total unchanged. Tap Cover on an over-budget row for a suggested move; repeating bills still due are left covered in suggestions. Pro adds daily guidance per budget, including with Lifetime. See the budget planner walkthrough.

SMS and voice help

See SMS setup videos and complete guide, automation troubleshooting and voice requirements. SMS automation does not read your inbox. Voice produces drafts, and supported languages/devices vary.

Privacy and deletion

Your ledger is on your device with optional private iCloud sync. See the complete policy for storage, processing, purchase records and deletion. For purchase-data deletion requests, email the developer.

App facts and free / Pro limits

Expense Tracker: SMS & Voice (TrackExpenses) is made by Mohamed Elatabany for iPhone and iPad with iOS/iPadOS 17 or later, and runs on Mac computers with Apple silicon. The app is in English, Arabic, German, Spanish and Brazilian Portuguese, with a full right-to-left Arabic interface. No app account or bank login is required. Optional Shortcuts automations import supported bank SMS, bank email alerts (through Apple’s Mail app) and Wallet purchases, and any bank alert can be pasted; the app does not read your Messages inbox or your email. Voice entry creates on-device drafts on compatible devices and languages. The free ledger includes unlimited manual transactions, budgets, current-period budget moves and Cover with the total unchanged, days left and spent/left amounts, budget alerts (at 80% when ahead of pace, and when over), bill reminders the morning before a repeating expense is due, historical stats, search with Spent and Income totals across every period, private iCloud sync across iPhone, iPad and Mac, 3 accounts, 2 recurring rules, 10 saved voice entries per calendar month per device and 10 successful automatic bank SMS or email imports per calendar month per device. Touching and holding the app icon offers Say an expense, New expense, Paste bank message and Ask your coach. The money coach reads the last budget month and suggests one limit. Today’s spending, a category, account balances, any named month, charts, files and typed logging are worked out on the device without using a question; open questions go to Anthropic’s Claude through the developer’s relay only after the user agrees, with the conversation so far, a summary of this month and last month and, when the model asks, totals for up to 13 months or up to 20 purchases with their date, shop, category and amount; never notes, receipts, account names or numbers, or balances (10 a month free per device). SMS alerts past the free allowance are kept but locked outside totals; upgrading to Pro adds them all, otherwise the next calendar month’s allowance releases as many as it covers. Nothing is dropped and saved history is never locked; when Pro or a trial ends, entries, accounts and repeating bills keep working. Pro adds unlimited voice entry, SMS and email imports, accounts and recurring rules, money coach challenges and more coach questions, a salary plan, forecasts, overall and per-budget daily guidance with repeating bills accounted for first, a daily budget for chosen categories optionally up to a date, what-if planning, Export & Share (CSV of any range and the full JSON backup; every export is Pro since 1.15.0), CSV import, backup restore, themes and alternate icons. Monthly and annual subscriptions and a one-time lifetime purchase are available. Spending calculations depend on your records and are not financial advice.
