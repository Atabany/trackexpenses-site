Bugs, questions, refunds, or a request to delete data. Please say which iPhone and which iOS version you're on — it usually saves a round trip.

I paid and Pro didn't unlock

Open TrackExpenses, open the Pro screen, and tap Restore purchase. That re-checks your Apple Account and is the fix in almost every case, including after reinstalling or moving to a new phone — as long as you use the same Apple Account you bought with.

If it still doesn't unlock, email us. Include the receipt Apple sent you and we'll sort it out. You will not be asked to pay twice.

How do I cancel?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple, not by us: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → TrackExpenses. Cancelling there stops the renewal, and Pro stays on until the period you already paid for runs out.

Lifetime is a single payment. There is nothing to cancel, and nothing renews.

What do I actually get for free?

The whole ledger. Unlimited transactions, all five transaction views, budgets, search, stats and export are free, and stay free. Pro adds more than three accounts, more than three recurring rules, and voice entry. A money app that holds your own numbers hostage doesn't deserve your trust, so this one doesn't.

My month doesn't start on the 1st

Neither does most people's. More → Period & Entry sets the day your month starts — if you're paid on the 25th, set 25 and every list, chart, budget and total in the app moves with it.

How do I set up SMS auto-import?

More → SMS Auto-Import walks you through it. It is a Shortcuts automation you create yourself, not access to your Messages — iOS never gives an app your inbox, and this one doesn't ask. Shortcuts hands the app one bank alert as it arrives; the app reads the amount, merchant and date, and never stores the message.

How do I get my data out?

More → Backup & Export writes a CSV for spreadsheets and a full-fidelity JSON backup of everything the app holds. You choose where either goes. Nothing is uploaded.

How do I delete my data?

Delete the app — that removes everything on the device. If iCloud sync was on, the synced copy lives in your Apple Account: remove it from your iOS settings under your name → iCloud → Manage Account Storage. For the purchase record Apple and RevenueCat hold, email us and we'll request its deletion on your behalf.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Everything except buying or restoring Pro works with no signal at all. The app makes no network requests of its own.
