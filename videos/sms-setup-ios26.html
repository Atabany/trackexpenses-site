Set up bank SMS imports on iOS 26

Before starting, open TrackExpenses once and finish onboarding. Use wording from your bank’s own purchase alerts: Purchase is only an example. Read the complete setup guide for filter selection, account matching, testing, privacy and troubleshooting.

Full video transcript

0:00 · Create a Message automation

In Shortcuts, open Automation. Tap +, then choose Message.

0:10 · Match your bank's wording

We use “purchase” as an example. Enter a phrase or currency code that appears in YOUR bank alerts.

0:25 · Run automatically

Leave Sender as Any Sender. Choose Run Immediately, then tap Next.

0:35 · Create a blank shortcut

Tap Create New Shortcut under Get Started.

0:45 · Add the bank import action

Search for Import Bank Message. Tap the TrackExpenses action.

0:57 · Tap the Message field

Tap the blue Message placeholder inside the import action. A variable bar opens at the bottom.

1:12 · Tap Select Variable

At the bottom, tap Select Variable. If the keyboard is open, look just above it.

1:24 · Choose Shortcut Input

Tap the blue Shortcut Input beneath “Receive messages as input”. This passes the incoming SMS to the action.

1:41 · Check the connection

The action now says Shortcut Input. Do not type those words yourself. Tap × to close the variable bar.

1:52 · Keep imports quiet

Tap the arrow beside the action. Turn Show When Run off.

2:04 · Save your automation

Tap the blue checkmark. Matching bank alerts will now reach TrackExpenses.

App facts and free / Pro limits

Expense Tracker: SMS & Voice (TrackExpenses) is made by Mohamed Elatabany for iPhone and iPad with iOS/iPadOS 17 or later. The app is in English and Arabic, with a full right-to-left Arabic interface. No app account or bank login is required. Optional Shortcuts automations import supported bank SMS and Wallet purchases; the app does not read your Messages inbox. Voice entry creates on-device drafts on compatible devices and languages. The free ledger includes unlimited manual transactions, budgets, historical stats, search, monthly CSV export, private iCloud sync, 3 accounts, 2 recurring rules, 10 saved voice entries per calendar month per device and 10 successful automatic bank SMS imports per calendar month per device. SMS alerts past the free allowance are kept but locked outside totals; upgrading to Pro adds them all, otherwise the next calendar month’s allowance releases as many as it covers. Nothing is dropped and saved history is never locked. Pro adds unlimited voice entry, SMS imports, accounts and recurring rules, forecasts, daily budget guidance, what-if planning, CSV import, date-range CSV exports, full JSON backup and restore, themes and alternate icons. Monthly and annual subscriptions and a one-time lifetime purchase are available. Spending calculations depend on your records and are not financial advice.
