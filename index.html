TrackExpenses

A money manager for iPhone: expenses, income, transfers, accounts, budgets, recurring transactions and stats — built around the month you're actually paid in, not the calendar's.

Your ledger, on your phone. No account to create, no bank login, no server of ours. What you record syncs through your own private iCloud and nowhere else, and every transaction you enter is free to keep, search and export.
