A Money Manager alternative with bank alerts, voice and Arabic.

Realbyte’s Money Manager is a widely used ledger you fill in by hand, with a custom month start, budgets and an Android version. Consider TrackExpenses if you want bank texts and emails to log themselves, purchases said aloud, free private iCloud sync across iPhone, iPad and Mac, or the app in Arabic. Stay with Money Manager if you need Android or one of its many other languages.

By Mohamed Elatabany · Updated November 2026 · Suggest a correction

This page is written by TrackExpenses’ developer, so read it as one side’s comparison, not an independent review. Money Manager’s facts come from its two App Store listings and its website, checked on 19 November 2026. Realbyte’s help center refused automated reading, so the few points that rest on it are marked. “Not documented” means I couldn’t confirm it, not that the feature is missing. TrackExpenses’ facts are checked against version 1.16.0.

When to keep Money Manager

  • You need Android, now or later, or you share records between an iPhone and an Android phone. TrackExpenses is not on Android.
  • Your iPhone can’t run iOS 17, or you use Vision Pro. Money Manager supports iOS 15.6 and Vision Pro.
  • You want a language TrackExpenses doesn’t have. Money Manager lists 19 languages; TrackExpenses has five.
  • You want separate weekly, monthly and annual budgets. TrackExpenses keeps one budget and works out a week or a year from it.
  • Manual entry already works for you. A habit you keep is worth more than a feature you don’t need, and Money Manager is widely used: 4.8 from about 20,000 US ratings when checked.

When to try TrackExpenses

  • Logging is the part you drop. A Shortcuts automation passes each bank text or email alert to the app, which files the amount, shop, card and category; or paste an alert, or say “coffee 15, taxi 40”. See bank SMS and email alerts and voice entry.
  • You want your ledger on every device without a sync subscription. iCloud sync is free and private, and includes the Mac.
  • You read Arabic, or prefer German, Spanish or Portuguese: the whole app, voice entry, bank alerts and the coach work in those languages.
  • You want the budget to warn you. Budgets carry a marker for today, alerts come when you run ahead of pace, and a daily budget says what you can spend today.
  • You want answers, not only charts. The money coach answers “what did I spend today?” or “how was September?” on your phone.

Both apps let the month start on payday, so that alone is not a reason to switch.

Moving your records

Keep your original Money Manager file. TrackExpenses imports CSV files with Pro, so save Money Manager’s Excel export as CSV in Numbers or Excel first. The importer tries to recognise a Money Manager export by its column names and sets the columns for you, but every column stays editable and you see every row before anything is saved. It asks when a date could be read two ways, such as 03/04/2026, and an import can be undone in one tap.

This page doesn’t promise that every Money Manager export imports cleanly. Try a small sample first and check dates, amounts, currencies, categories, and that the shop name and the note land where you expect. A move between your own accounts should come in as a transfer, not a new purchase. More in the CSV export and import guide.

Questions

Can I import my Money Manager data into TrackExpenses?

Possibly, with Pro. Save Money Manager’s Excel export as CSV, then use CSV import in More, Backup and Export: you check the columns and every row before anything is saved, and an import can be undone. Try a small sample first, because not every export has been tested.

Does TrackExpenses charge for sync?

No. Sync goes through your own private iCloud and is free for everyone, across iPhone, iPad and Mac.

Is TrackExpenses on Android?

No. It is on the App Store for iPhone, iPad and Mac with Apple silicon. If you need Android, Money Manager has an Android version.

Do both apps support a month that starts on payday?

Yes. Money Manager can change the start date of the month, and TrackExpenses lets the month start on any day from 1 to 31 for every list, budget and chart.

App facts and free / Pro limits

Expense Tracker: SMS & Voice (TrackExpenses) is made by Mohamed Elatabany for iPhone and iPad with iOS/iPadOS 17 or later, and runs on Mac computers with Apple silicon. The app is in English, Arabic, German, Spanish and Brazilian Portuguese, with a full right-to-left Arabic interface. No app account or bank login is required. Optional Shortcuts automations import supported bank SMS, bank email alerts (through Apple’s Mail app) and Wallet purchases, and a bank SMS or email can be pasted; the app does not read your Messages inbox or your email. Voice entry creates on-device drafts on compatible devices and languages. The free ledger includes unlimited manual transactions, budgets, current-period budget moves and Cover with the total unchanged, days left and spent/left amounts, budget alerts (at 80% when ahead of pace, and when over), bill reminders the morning before a repeating expense is due, historical stats, search with Spent and Income totals across every period, private iCloud sync across iPhone, iPad and Mac, 3 accounts, 2 recurring rules, 10 saved voice entries per calendar month per device and 10 successful automatic bank SMS or email imports per calendar month per device. Touching and holding the app icon offers Say an expense, New expense, Paste bank message and Ask your coach. The money coach reads the last budget month and suggests one limit. Today’s spending, a category, account balances, any named month, charts, files and typed logging are worked out on the device without using a question; open questions go to Anthropic’s Claude through the developer’s relay only after the user agrees, with the conversation so far, a summary of this month and last month and, when the model asks, totals for up to 13 months or up to 20 purchases with their date, shop, category and amount; never notes, receipts, account names or numbers, or balances (10 a month free per device). SMS alerts past the free allowance are kept but locked outside totals; upgrading to Pro adds them all, otherwise the next calendar month’s allowance releases as many as it covers. Nothing is dropped and saved history is never locked; when Pro or a trial ends, entries, accounts and repeating bills keep working. Pro adds unlimited voice entry, SMS and email imports, accounts and recurring rules, money coach challenges and more coach questions, a salary plan, forecasts, overall and per-budget daily guidance with repeating bills accounted for first, a daily budget for chosen categories optionally up to a date, what-if planning, Export & Share (CSV of any range and the full JSON backup; every export is Pro since 1.15.0), CSV import, backup restore, themes and alternate icons. Monthly and annual subscriptions and a one-time lifetime purchase are available. Spending calculations depend on your records and are not financial advice.
