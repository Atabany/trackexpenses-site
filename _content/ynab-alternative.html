A YNAB alternative for payday months, several currencies and no bank link.

YNAB is a complete zero-based budgeting system with optional bank import and a budget you can share. Consider TrackExpenses if your budget month starts on payday, you spend in more than one currency, you want the app in Arabic, German, Spanish or Portuguese, or you would rather log from bank texts and your voice than link a bank. YNAB’s own support pages document the first three as things it doesn’t do. Stay with YNAB if you want bank import, a shared budget or its method.

When YNAB is the better choice

  • You want transactions to arrive from your bank by themselves and you bank in the US, Canada, the UK or the EU. TrackExpenses never connects to a bank.
  • You share money with a partner or family. YNAB shares one budget with up to 6 people; TrackExpenses has no shared budgets.
  • You want a web app, Android or Apple Watch. TrackExpenses is on iPhone, iPad and Mac only.
  • You want a strict zero-based method and the way YNAB builds the whole app around it. TrackExpenses’ salary plan shows what is left to plan, but its budgets are simpler and unspent money doesn’t roll over.

When to try TrackExpenses

  • Your month runs from payday. Set the start day once and every list, budget and chart follows it. See the payday budget guide.
  • You spend in more than one currency and want one budget: each entry keeps the currency and rate you paid, and every total converts to your main currency. See multi-currency.
  • You want the app in Arabic, German, Spanish or Portuguese, including voice entry, bank alerts and the coach.
  • You won’t link a bank, but don’t want to type everything. Bank texts and emails can log themselves through a Shortcuts automation, alerts can be pasted, and purchases can be said aloud. See bank SMS and email alerts.
  • You want no account and a free ledger. Manual entry, budgets, moving money between budgets, alerts, reminders and iCloud sync are free; YNAB is subscription only.
  • You want to export from your phone. Export & Share writes a CSV or a full backup on the iPhone, with Pro.

Moving your records

Export your register from YNAB’s web app as CSV and keep the file. TrackExpenses Pro imports CSV: you check which column is which, including files with separate money-in and money-out columns, answer a question if a date could be read two ways, see every row before anything is saved, and undo the import in one tap if something looks wrong. YNAB’s plan targets and notes aren’t in its export, so set up your budgets in TrackExpenses by hand. This page doesn’t promise every YNAB file imports cleanly; try a small sample first. Questions

Can my budget month start on payday in YNAB?

Not as a setting. YNAB’s support says it uses a monthly plan to accommodate all pay cycles. TrackExpenses lets the month start on any day from 1 to 31.

Can I keep several currencies in one budget?

YNAB’s support says it doesn’t convert currencies within a plan and suggests one plan per currency. TrackExpenses keeps each entry in the currency you paid, at your own rate, and converts every total to one main currency.

Is TrackExpenses a zero-based budgeting app?

Not in YNAB’s sense. With Pro, its salary plan puts your income at the top and shows what is left to plan after spending and savings, but budgets are per period and unspent money doesn’t roll over.

Does TrackExpenses connect to my bank like YNAB?

No. It has no bank connection. Bank texts and emails can log themselves through a Shortcuts automation you set up, and any alert can be pasted.

Can I share TrackExpenses with my partner?

No. iCloud sync joins the devices on one Apple Account, and there are no shared budgets. YNAB shares with up to 6 people.
