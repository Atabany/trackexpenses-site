# Log Expenses with Siri, Widgets and Shortcuts on iPhone

You can track spending on iPhone without opening an app each time: ask Siri what you can spend today, keep your budget on the Home Screen or Lock Screen, and log a regular purchase with one tap from a widget, Control Center or the Action button. TrackExpenses provides all of these, and More → Siri, Shortcuts & Widgets in the app shows each one with your own figures.

## The method that works anywhere

Before any app, iPhone already gives you two quick habits. A Notes checklist pinned to the top of Notes holds the day's purchases, and a Reminders list with a daily alert at the end of the day prompts you to write them down. Both work, and both leave the arithmetic to you: neither knows your budget, how many days are left before payday, or whether today's coffee leaves room for tomorrow.

The step that makes quick logging worth it is a number you can act on in the moment. A budget is only useful at the till if you know what is left for today, not just what is left for the month. That is the figure TrackExpenses puts on the Lock Screen and gives to Siri.

## Doing it in TrackExpenses

**Ask Siri.** Say the app's name in the phrase. "What can I spend today in TrackExpenses" answers with what is left of today's daily budget: the period's budget after repeating bills, shared across the days left. "How much did I spend on Groceries in TrackExpenses" names the category's spending against its budget, using your own category names. "How's my budget in TrackExpenses" and "What's my net worth in TrackExpenses" work the same way. Siri asks you to unlock the iPhone before it reads out any figure.

**Log by voice.** "Tell TrackExpenses what I spent" asks what you spent; answer naturally, such as "coffee 15 and taxi 40 with cash". Siri reads back what it heard and asks before saving. Each entry counts toward the free voice allowance of 10 saved voice entries a calendar month; Pro makes voice entry unlimited. "Log an expense in TrackExpenses" asks for an amount instead and is not counted.

**Log a template with one tap.** Turn on Save as template when you add an entry you repeat, such as the morning coffee or the bus fare. The Quick Log widget shows your templates as buttons: a tap logs the template's amount, category and account without opening the app. The widget marks the tile and says the entry is waiting until the app next opens, when it lands in the ledger. "Log my Coffee in TrackExpenses" does the same through Siri.

**Widgets.** Touch and hold an empty part of the Home Screen, tap Edit, then Add Widget, and search for TrackExpenses. There are seven: Today (what is left to spend today), Budget (what is left this period with a marker for today's place in it), Quick Log, Category Budgets, Spending Overview (today, the last two weeks, income and net balance), Accounts (net worth and balances) and Quick Add (type, say or paste). Quick Log, Category Budgets and Accounts can be set to one template, category or account: touch and hold the widget and choose Edit Widget.

**Lock Screen.** Touch and hold the Lock Screen, tap Customize, choose the Lock Screen, then tap the widget area and pick TrackExpenses. Today and Budget fit as a ring or a line of text; Quick Log fits as a button. On an iPhone with a passcode, the figures stay hidden until you unlock it.

**Control Center and the Action button.** On iOS 18 and later, open Control Center, tap the plus at the top, choose Add a Control and search for TrackExpenses. Five controls are available: Quick Log, Today's Budget, Add an Expense, Say an Expense and Paste an Expense. Quick Log asks which template to log when you add it. On an iPhone with an Action button, Settings → Action Button → Controls lets you choose one of them for a single press.

## A worked example

Say your monthly budget is 4,000 and rent of 1,500 is set up as a repeating bill. Twenty days are left, and 1,300 has gone on everyday spending so far. The daily budget is (4,000 − 1,500 − 1,300) ÷ 20 = 60. At lunch you ask Siri what you can spend today; it answers that you have 60 left, out of 60. You buy a sandwich and tap the Lunch tile on the Quick Log widget, which logs 25 under Food. The Today widget on your Lock Screen then shows 35 left today. Spend nothing more, and tomorrow's daily budget rises a little, because today's unspent 35 is shared across the days that remain.

## Limits and common mistakes

Siri's spending answers need a budget. With no budget set, "what can I spend today" tells you what you have spent today and suggests setting one. The daily budget figures on widgets and in Siri are part of Pro; free users see today's spending in their place, and "budget used up" is always shown free.

A Quick Log tap made while the app is closed waits in a queue and is added the next time you open TrackExpenses. Totals on other widgets include it from then. If you tap by mistake, delete the entry in the app as you would any other.

Transfers between your own accounts need the app. Siri logs purchases and income; a sentence that moves money from cash to savings is left out and Siri says so.

Control Center controls need iOS 18 or later. Widgets, Siri and Shortcuts work from iOS 17.

## Frequently asked questions

Are the widgets free? Yes. Every widget, control and Siri action is free to use. The daily budget figure they show is part of Pro, and voice entry includes 10 free saved entries a month.

Does TrackExpenses send my spending anywhere to answer Siri? No. The answers are worked out on your iPhone from a summary the app keeps on the device, the same one the widgets read.

Why does my Quick Log widget say an entry is waiting? The tap was saved while the app was closed. Open TrackExpenses and it is added to the ledger at once.

Can I choose which category a widget follows? Yes. Touch and hold the Category Budgets widget, choose Edit Widget and pick a category. Accounts and Quick Log work the same way.
