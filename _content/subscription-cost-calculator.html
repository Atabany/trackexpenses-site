How it is calculated

Yearly cost = price × times billed in a year. A weekly price is multiplied by 52, a monthly one by 12, one billed every three months by 4, and a yearly one counts once. The monthly figure is the yearly total divided by 12, which is why a yearly plan of 30 shows as 2.50 a month. Five years is the yearly total times five.

The five-year figure assumes prices stay where they are. They usually rise, so read it as the least you would pay, not a forecast. The list under the result sorts your subscriptions by yearly cost, biggest first: the top two or three are where a decision changes the total.

Find every subscription you pay for

On iPhone, open Settings, tap your name, then tap Subscriptions. That list shows what is billed through Apple, with each renewal date and price; Apple's subscriptions help page shows the same steps and how to cancel there. Anything you signed up for on a website is not in that list, so go through one full month of card and bank statements as well, and look back a year for yearly renewals, which are the easiest to forget.

Monthly or yearly plan?

Divide the yearly price by 12 and compare it with the monthly price. A plan that costs 10 a month or 96 a year works out at 8 a month on the yearly plan, 24 less over the year. That saving is real only if you keep the subscription all year: cancel after three months and the yearly plan cost 96 where three monthly payments would have cost 30.

Keep them in your ledger

A total you worked out once goes stale the next time a price changes. In TrackExpenses a subscription is a repeating transaction: it is written into your ledger on its day without you entering it, and a changed price is one edit to the rule. Two repeating rules are free and Pro makes them unlimited. Stats → Overview then shows your fixed costs as a month and as a year.

Questions

How do I see my subscriptions on iPhone?

Open Settings, tap your name, then tap Subscriptions. The list shows subscriptions billed through Apple. Ones you pay for directly, by card or through your bank, appear only on your statements.

Is a yearly plan cheaper than paying monthly?

Usually, if you keep it for the whole year. Divide the yearly price by 12 and compare it with the monthly price. A yearly plan you stop using after three months costs more than three monthly payments would have.

How much should I spend on subscriptions?

There is no fixed rule. In a 50/30/20 budget most subscriptions are wants, so they share the 30% with eating out, shopping and entertainment. Seeing the yearly total beside your pay is what makes the decision clear.
