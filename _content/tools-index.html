Free budget calculators

Three small calculators for the questions a budget starts with. They run in your browser: nothing you type is sent or saved, and there is nothing to sign up for.

UNTIL PAYDAY

Daily budget until payday

What is left of your budget, divided by the days until you are paid again, today included.

PLAN THE MONTH

50/30/20 budget calculator

Your take-home pay split into needs, wants and savings, per paycheck, per month and per year, with shares you can change.

FIXED COSTS

Subscription cost calculator

Every subscription added up as a month, a year and five years, sorted by what costs the most.

How these calculators are made

Each page states its formula and prints a reference table, so you can check a result by hand or use the page with JavaScript switched off. Amounts carry no currency symbol: use one currency for everything you enter. The results are arithmetic on the figures you type, not financial advice.

When you want the same answers from your real spending, TrackExpenses works them out from your ledger, and the guides explain each method step by step.
