What is left to spend per day?

Subtract spending and any extra commitments from your period budget, then divide by the days left, including today. This is budget arithmetic; an unrecorded bill or purchase can change the answer.

Daily budget until payday

All figures stay in this browser. They are not sent or saved. Use one currency for every amount.

Example: 1,200 − 480 − 120 = 600 remaining. Over 10 days including today, that is 60.00 per day.

The formula and its limits

Daily amount = max(0, budget − recorded spending − extra commitments) ÷ days left. We include today and the last day. A period ending today has one day left. A date in the past produces an error instead of an invented allowance. The display rounds to two decimal places; the calculation keeps the unrounded result.

Only subtract extra commitments once. If next week's rent is already included in recorded spending, leave it out of the extra-commitments field. This calculator offers an optional reserve you choose; it is not an exact replica of every TrackExpenses forecasting scenario.
