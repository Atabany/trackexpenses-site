What stays where

  • No bank login: entries come from you, your voice, alerts you paste, or bank texts and emails you choose to pass in through Shortcuts.
  • No account: nothing to sign up for; no email required.
  • On your devices: the ledger is stored on your iPhone or iPad. Optional iCloud sync uses the private part of your own iCloud to keep it the same on your iPhone, iPad and Mac.
  • Messages aren't kept: an imported alert is read and discarded; only the transaction is saved.
  • Voice stays local: speech is recognized on the device.
  • The coach asks first: questions the phone can answer, charts and logging stay on the device. An open question goes to an AI only after you agree, and never with your notes, receipts, account names or numbers, or balances. What leaves your phone.

App Lock

More → App Lock sets a passcode, with Face ID to unlock. The passcode protects this device only and never leaves it.

When Pro or a trial ends

Nothing you saved is locked. Your entries, accounts and repeating bills stay as they are and keep working, even past the free limits of 3 accounts and 2 repeating rules. A short note says what changed: a Pro theme goes back to the standard one and a Pro app icon to the main icon, and both come back by themselves if you get Pro again.

Your data, portable

Your records stay in the app, free, and are never locked. Pro adds Export & Share (a CSV of any span and the full JSON backup), restoring a backup and CSV import, so you can move in or out without losing history. More → Backup & Export → Erase all data deletes everything from this device and every device that syncs with your iCloud. The privacy policy explains each kind of data in detail.

Questions

Does TrackExpenses connect to my bank?

No. There is no bank connection and no bank login. Transactions come from manual entry, voice, alerts you paste, or bank messages you choose to pass in.

Do I need an account?

No. There is no app account. Sync, if you turn it on, uses your own private iCloud.

Can I lock the app?

Yes. App Lock sets a passcode with Face ID unlock, and it is free.

What happens to my data if Pro ends?

Nothing you saved is locked. Entries, accounts and repeating bills stay and keep working. A Pro theme and app icon go back to the standard ones and return if you get Pro again.
