What you can spend today, worked out for you.

TrackExpenses turns your budget into one figure for today: what the period has left after its repeating bills, shared across the days to go. It sits at the top of your daily list, holds still all day, and says plainly when the budget is used up. You never type it in.

How today’s figure is worked out

  1. Start from the period’s budget. The total you set in More → Budgets, for a month that can start on your payday.
  2. Take out repeating bills. Rent, subscriptions and every other repeating expense in the period come out first, paid or still to come, so a bill due next week is already set aside.
  3. Take out what you have spent. Everything spent before today, plus anything you logged for a later date.
  4. Share the rest across the days left, today included, rounded down so the figure never promises more than there is.

StepAmount
Monthly budget4,000.00

Spend 40.00 today and the card says 60.00 left today. Tomorrow’s figure is worked out again from what is left: 1,560.00 across 15 days is 104.00. Spending less today raises tomorrow’s figure, and spending more lowers it. Tap the card to see the working step by step and what tomorrow looks like.

It holds still all day

Today’s figure is worked out before today’s spending, so it doesn’t shrink each time you buy something: today’s purchases count against it, and the card shows what you spent and what is left. It turns over at midnight. The figure always uses your budget’s own period, so a week or a year on screen never cuts a month’s rent into the wrong slice.

When the budget is used up

Once nothing is left to share, the card stops being about today. It says you are over your monthly budget and by how much, shows a bar with a notch where the budget ended, and counts the days left and any bills still due. Today’s budget sheet says when a fresh budget starts. This part is free, always: a used-up budget is a fact you should see without paying.

A daily amount for each budget

In Stats → Budget, every category budget shows its own daily amount for the days left, worked out the same way: that category’s repeating bills first, then what it has spent. A category made only of repeating bills shows no daily amount. See the budget planner for moving money between budgets and covering an overspend.

A daily budget for a trip or the household

With Pro, today’s figure can come from the budgets you choose instead of the whole budget. Open today’s budget, tap Daily budget for and pick Chosen categories: one category, such as Travel, or several read as one, such as Groceries and Household. Only categories with a budget of their own are listed.

Turn on Stop on a date for a trip: what those budgets have left is shared across the days up to that date, inside the current budget period, instead of to the end of the month. With 3,000.00 left and 10 days to go, that is 300.00 a day. The day after the last day, the daily budget is your whole budget again. Every place that shows the focused figure names the budgets it comes from, such as “Travel · until 20 Nov”.

This changes only which daily figure the app shows you. Your budgets stay exactly as they are, and widgets and Siri keep to the whole budget. The choice is kept on the device where you made it.

Questions

Is the daily budget free?

The daily figure, the sheet that shows how it is worked out, a daily amount for each budget and the daily budget for chosen categories are part of Pro, including Lifetime. When the budget is used up, the app says so for free, with how far over you are and the days left.

Can I type my own daily budget?

No. It is always your period’s budget, minus repeating bills and what you have spent, shared across the days left. To spend less each day, lower the budget.

Why didn’t today’s figure change after I bought something?

It is worked out before today’s spending, so it holds still all day and today’s purchases count against it. The card shows what you spent today and what is left. Spending less today raises tomorrow’s figure.

Can I see a daily budget for a trip?

Yes, with Pro. In today’s budget, tap Daily budget for, choose the categories such as Travel, and turn on Stop on a date. What those budgets have left is shared across the days up to that date. The day after, the daily budget is your whole budget again.

Do widgets and Siri show the chosen categories?

No. Widgets and Siri keep to the whole budget. Choosing categories only changes the figure in the app, and your budgets stay as they are.
