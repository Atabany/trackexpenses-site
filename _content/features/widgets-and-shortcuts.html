Widgets

  • Budget: what is left to spend this period — Home Screen and Lock Screen sizes.
  • Spending Overview: today's spending and the period's income and expenses.
  • Quick Add: add an expense by typing, voice or a bank message; a Lock Screen microphone opens Say it.

Control Center

On iOS 18 and later, add Say an Expense and Paste an Expense to Control Center or the Lock Screen. Copy a bank alert, swipe down, tap Paste an Expense and the draft is ready.

Siri and Shortcuts

Actions include Log an Expense, Log Income, Say an Expense, Paste an Expense, Budget Remaining, Import Bank Message and Import Wallet Transaction. Ask Siri “What's left in TrackExpenses”, or build a Wallet automation that logs Apple Pay purchases on selected cards (see the Apple Pay guide).

Quick Add and templates

The Quick Add button holds two shortcuts: paste a bank message to fill the amount, merchant and date, or tap a saved template for the things you buy every week. Turn on “Save as template” in the entry sheet to make one. A daily reminder (More → Daily Reminder) nudges you if you like a habit.

Questions

Are widgets free?

Yes. All widgets, Control Center buttons, Shortcuts actions and templates are free. The voice entry they open requires Pro.

Which iOS do I need for Control Center buttons?

Control Center and Lock Screen controls need iOS 18 or later. Widgets and Shortcuts actions work from iOS 17.
