FEATURE · BUDGETS · FREE + PRO

A budget that starts on payday and knows what day it is.

TrackExpenses lets your budget month start on any day from the 1st to the 31st, shows every budget bar with a marker for today, and — with Pro — turns what is left into a daily budget allowance, a forecast and what-if plans.

Real app screens with example records.

Your month, not the calendar's

Paid on the 25th? Set More → Period & Entry to start on the 25th and every list, chart, budget and total runs from 25th to 24th. Days past the end of a short month are clamped, so “31” simply means the last day.

Pacing, not just totals

Sixty percent spent means nothing until you know where the month is. Every budget bar pairs the amount spent with a notch for today, so you can see at a glance whether you are ahead of or behind the month. Set a total budget and per-category budgets under More → Budgets, weekly, monthly or yearly.

What Pro adds

  • Daily budget allowance: what is left, divided by the days left including today. If it reaches zero you see “Budget used up” — free, always.
  • Forecast: where this period is heading at your current pace.
  • What-if plans: try a daily target or a planned purchase and see the effect before you spend.

Historical comparisons, averages and category changes stay free. Try the arithmetic without the app in the daily budget calculator.

Questions

Can the budget month start on my payday?

Yes. Choose any start day from 1 to 31 under More, Period and Entry. It applies to the whole ledger, not just one budget.

Is budgeting free?

Yes. Total and category budgets, the today marker and the payday month are free. Daily allowance, forecasts and what-if plans are Pro.

Does it support biweekly pay?

The setting is a fixed monthly start day. Weekly and yearly budget views exist, but there is no rolling two-week pay cycle.

App facts and free / Pro limits

Expense Tracker: SMS & Voice (TrackExpenses) is made by Mohamed Elatabany for iPhone and iPad with iOS/iPadOS 17 or later. The interface is English. No app account or bank login is required. Optional Shortcuts automations import supported bank SMS and Wallet purchases; the app does not read your Messages inbox. Voice entry creates on-device drafts on compatible devices and languages and requires Pro. The free ledger includes unlimited manual transactions, budgets, historical stats, search, monthly CSV export, private iCloud sync, 3 accounts, 3 recurring rules and 20 successful automatic bank SMS imports per calendar month per device. Pro adds unlimited SMS imports, accounts and recurring rules, forecasts, daily budget guidance, what-if planning, voice entry, CSV import, date-range CSV exports, full JSON backup and restore, themes and alternate icons. Monthly and annual subscriptions and a one-time lifetime purchase are available. Spending calculations depend on your records and are not financial advice.
