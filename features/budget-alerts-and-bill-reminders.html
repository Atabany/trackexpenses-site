FEATURE · ALERTS & REMINDERS · FREE

A heads-up before the budget runs out, and before a bill is due.

Two notifications, both free and both off until you turn them on. Budget alerts tell you when a budget is running ahead of its pace or has gone over. Bill reminders arrive the morning before rent, a subscription or any other repeating expense comes out.

Real app screens with example records, from an earlier version.

Budget alerts

Open More → Budgets and turn on Budget alerts; iOS asks once whether TrackExpenses may send notifications. From then on the app watches the whole budget and every category budget in the current budget period.

  • Ahead of pace at 80%: when a budget passes 80% while spending is ahead of the month, past the marker for today. A typical alert reads “Groceries budget: 82% spent. AED 171.00 left for 12 days.”
  • Over, once: when spending passes a budget. “Over the Food budget. AED 40.00 over, with 12 days left.”
  • Several at once, one notification: when a few budgets cross a mark together, for example after a batch of bank alerts, you get one notification naming them.
  • No burst when you switch it on: it starts from where your budgets stand and tells you about what happens next, not about the month so far.
  • News again only if it changes: each mark is said once a period. If a budget drops back under it, because you moved money in or deleted an entry, crossing it again is news again.
  • Bills aren’t a warning: a budget filled only by repeating bills, such as rent on the 1st, never gets the 80% alert. It still tells you if that budget goes over.

Why not a flat 80%? Eighty percent spent with two days left is a budget working as planned. Every budget bar in the app pairs what you spent with where today falls, and the alerts follow the same rule. Tapping an alert opens Stats → Budget. A purchase that a bank alert automation imports is checked straight away, so an alert about it arrives with it rather than the next time you open the app.

Bill reminders

Open More → Recurring and turn on Remind me the day before. The switch appears once you have a repeating expense. A Next reminder row then says which one comes next.

  • 9:00 the morning before: early enough to move money the same day. “Rent is due tomorrow. AED 4,300.00 from Main Bank · Wednesday, 25 November.”
  • One notification a day: bills due on the same day come together, biggest first, with the total, such as “3 bills are due tomorrow”.
  • Expenses only: income never gets a reminder, and an expense that repeats more often than once a week is left out, so the one about the rent is never lost in daily noise.
  • Never late: a reminder whose moment has passed is not sent afterwards.

Tapping a reminder opens the Recurring screen. Reminders follow your repeating rules: add, change or stop a rule and the reminders change with it. More on rules in accounts, cards and recurring and the subscriptions and recurring bills guide.

Set on each device

Notifications belong to a phone, so both switches are kept on the device where you turn them on, even with iCloud sync. If notifications are off for TrackExpenses in iOS Settings, the switch shows Open Settings and says why nothing can be sent.

Free or Pro

Both are free. Bill reminders cover your repeating expenses: 2 repeating rules are free, and Pro makes them unlimited. To see what is left for today rather than wait for an alert, see the daily budget. Step by step: get an alert before you overspend and get reminded before bills are due.

Questions

Are budget alerts and bill reminders free?

Yes, both are free. Bill reminders cover your repeating expenses: 2 repeating rules are free, and Pro makes them unlimited.

Why didn’t I get an alert at 80%?

The 80% alert comes only when spending is ahead of the month’s pace, past the marker for today. 80% spent with two days left is a budget working as planned. A budget filled only by repeating bills never gets it. Also check that notifications are allowed for TrackExpenses in iOS Settings.

What time do bill reminders arrive?

At 9:00 in the morning, the day before the bill is due. Bills due on the same day come in one notification, biggest first.

Will I get a reminder for my salary?

No. Only repeating expenses get reminders. Income never does, and expenses that repeat more often than once a week are left out.

Do I have to turn them on on every device?

Yes. Notifications belong to each iPhone or iPad, so each switch is set on the device where you want the notifications.

App facts and free / Pro limits

Expense Tracker: SMS & Voice (TrackExpenses) is made by Mohamed Elatabany for iPhone and iPad with iOS/iPadOS 17 or later, and runs on Mac computers with Apple silicon. The app is in English, Arabic, German, Spanish and Brazilian Portuguese, with a full right-to-left Arabic interface. No app account or bank login is required. Optional Shortcuts automations import supported bank SMS, bank email alerts (through Apple’s Mail app) and Wallet purchases, and a bank SMS or email can be pasted; the app does not read your Messages inbox or your email. Voice entry creates on-device drafts on compatible devices and languages. The free ledger includes unlimited manual transactions, budgets, current-period budget moves and Cover with the total unchanged, days left and spent/left amounts, budget alerts (at 80% when ahead of pace, and when over), bill reminders the morning before a repeating expense is due, historical stats, search with Spent and Income totals across every period, private iCloud sync across iPhone, iPad and Mac, 3 accounts, 2 recurring rules, 10 saved voice entries per calendar month per device and 10 successful automatic bank SMS or email imports per calendar month per device. Touching and holding the app icon offers Say an expense, New expense, Paste bank message and Ask your coach. The money coach reads the last budget month and suggests one limit. Today’s spending, a category, account balances, any named month, charts, files and typed logging are worked out on the device without using a question; open questions go to Anthropic’s Claude through the developer’s relay only after the user agrees, with the conversation so far, a summary of this month and last month and, when the model asks, totals for up to 13 months or up to 20 purchases with their date, shop, category and amount; never notes, receipts, account names or numbers, or balances (10 a month free per device). SMS alerts past the free allowance are kept but locked outside totals; upgrading to Pro adds them all, otherwise the next calendar month’s allowance releases as many as it covers. Nothing is dropped and saved history is never locked; when Pro or a trial ends, entries, accounts and repeating bills keep working. Pro adds unlimited voice entry, SMS and email imports, accounts and recurring rules, money coach challenges and more coach questions, a salary plan, forecasts, overall and per-budget daily guidance with repeating bills accounted for first, a daily budget for chosen categories optionally up to a date, what-if planning, Export & Share (CSV of any range and the full JSON backup; every export is Pro since 1.15.0), CSV import, backup restore, themes and alternate icons. Monthly and annual subscriptions and a one-time lifetime purchase are available. Spending calculations depend on your records and are not financial advice.
