How the paydays are counted

Every two weeks: each payday is 14 days after the one before, counted from the date you entered. A year has 52 weeks and one day (two in a leap year), so it holds 26 such paydays and, once in a while, 27. Twelve months of two paydays make only 24, which is why two months of the year, sometimes three, carry a third.

Every week: each payday is 7 days after the one before. Most months have four and four or five months have five.

Once or twice a month: the payday is the same day number every month. A day that a short month does not have, such as the 31st in June or the 30th in February, moves to that month's last day. If you choose it, a payday on a Saturday or Sunday moves to the Friday before; when that Friday is in the month before, the table keeps it in its own month's row and writes the date with its month. Employers and countries differ here: some pay on the working day after, and where the weekend is Friday and Saturday the rule is different again, so check your contract or a past payslip.

Days until payday are counted from today to the next payday on the calendar. If today is a payday, the answer is zero and the result names the one after.

What to do with a third paycheck

Most monthly bills are sized for two paychecks. The simplest plan when you are paid every two weeks is to budget every month on two, then decide in advance what a third one is for: a yearly bill such as insurance, a savings goal, or paying a debt down faster. Decided ahead of time it is a plan; left open it tends to disappear into ordinary spending.

The same reasoning works the other way round. Twice-a-month pay is 24 paydays a year, so there is never a third paycheck, but each one is a little larger than a two-weekly one for the same yearly salary.

A budget month that starts on payday

If you are paid once a month, TrackExpenses can start your month on that day: choose a start day from 1 to 31 in More → Period & Entry, and your transactions, budgets and charts all run from payday to the day before the next. The start day is a fixed day of the month; the app does not follow a two-weekly schedule by itself. With Pro, the salary plan has a place for extra income that belongs to one month only, which is where a third paycheck fits.

Questions

How many paychecks are there in a year when you are paid every two weeks?

Usually 26. A year is 52 weeks and one day, so every eleven years or so a two-weekly schedule fits 27 paydays into one calendar year. In 2027 that happens when the first payday is Friday 1 January.

Which months have three paychecks in 2027?

For pay every second Friday: January, July and December if your first payday of the year is 1 January, or April and October if it is 8 January. For another weekday, enter one of your paydays in the calculator.

What is the difference between biweekly and twice a month?

Biweekly pay comes every 14 days, 26 times a year, on the same weekday. Twice-a-month pay comes on two fixed dates, 24 times a year, on changing weekdays. For the same yearly salary a biweekly paycheck is smaller, and two or three months a year contain a third one.
