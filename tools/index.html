Free budget calculators

Three small calculators for the questions a budget starts with. They run in your browser: nothing you type is sent or saved, and there is nothing to sign up for.

UNTIL PAYDAY

Daily budget until payday

What is left of your budget, divided by the days until you are paid again, today included.

PLAN THE MONTH

50/30/20 budget calculator

Your take-home pay split into needs, wants and savings, per paycheck, per month and per year, with shares you can change.

FIXED COSTS

Subscription cost calculator

Every subscription added up as a month, a year and five years, sorted by what costs the most.

How these calculators are made

Each page states its formula and prints a reference table, so you can check a result by hand or use the page with JavaScript switched off. Amounts carry no currency symbol: use one currency for everything you enter. The results are arithmetic on the figures you type, not financial advice.

When you want the same answers from your real spending, TrackExpenses works them out from your ledger, and the guides explain each method step by step.

Expense Tracker: SMS & Voice for iPhone and iPad.

App facts and free / Pro limits

Expense Tracker: SMS & Voice (TrackExpenses) is made by Mohamed Elatabany for iPhone and iPad with iOS/iPadOS 17 or later. The app is in English and Arabic, with a full right-to-left Arabic interface. No app account or bank login is required. Optional Shortcuts automations import supported bank SMS and Wallet purchases; the app does not read your Messages inbox. Voice entry creates on-device drafts on compatible devices and languages. The free ledger includes unlimited manual transactions, budgets, current-period budget moves and Cover with the total unchanged, days left and spent/left amounts, historical stats, search, monthly CSV export, private iCloud sync, 3 accounts, 2 recurring rules, 10 saved voice entries per calendar month per device and 10 successful automatic bank SMS imports per calendar month per device. The money coach reads the last budget month and suggests one limit. Questions it can answer by rule, charts, files and typed logging are worked out on the device; open questions go to Anthropic’s Claude through the developer’s relay only after the user agrees, with the conversation so far, a summary of this month and last month and, when the model asks, totals for up to 13 months or up to 20 purchases with their date, shop, category and amount; never notes, receipts, account names or numbers, or balances (10 a month free per device). SMS alerts past the free allowance are kept but locked outside totals; upgrading to Pro adds them all, otherwise the next calendar month’s allowance releases as many as it covers. Nothing is dropped and saved history is never locked. Pro adds unlimited voice entry, SMS imports, accounts and recurring rules, money coach challenges and more coach questions, a salary plan, forecasts, overall and per-budget daily guidance with repeating bills accounted for first, what-if planning, CSV import, date-range CSV exports, full JSON backup and restore, themes and alternate icons. Monthly and annual subscriptions and a one-time lifetime purchase are available. Spending calculations depend on your records and are not financial advice.
