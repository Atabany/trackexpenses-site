TrackExpenses questions, answered.

Plain answers to what people ask before and after installing Expense Tracker: SMS & Voice (TrackExpenses): bank logins, messages, banks and countries, free and Pro, the money coach, languages and devices. Each answer describes version 1.16.0, the version on the App Store.

Privacy and your data

Does TrackExpenses connect to my bank?

No. There is no bank connection and no bank login. Entries come from typing, voice, alerts you paste, or Shortcuts automations you set up that pass bank texts, bank emails or Apple Pay purchases to the app.

How to track expenses on iPhone
Do I need to create an account?

No. There is no TrackExpenses account and no email sign-up. If you turn on sync, it goes through your own private iCloud.

Does TrackExpenses read my Messages or my email?

No. iOS doesn’t let an app read your inbox. A Shortcuts automation that you create passes each matching bank text or email to the app, one at a time. The app keeps what it read from the alert, never the message itself.

Where is my data stored?

On your iPhone or iPad, and in the private part of your own iCloud if sync is on. The developer runs no server for your ledger. The one exception is a typed question you send to the money coach’s AI after agreeing to it.

How do I delete all my data?

Open More, then Backup and Export, then Erase all data. It deletes your transactions, accounts, categories, budgets, recurring rules, templates, period notes and settings from this device and every device that syncs with your iCloud, and it can’t be undone. Deleting the app alone leaves the synced copy in your iCloud.

Can I lock the app?

Yes, for free. More, then App Lock, sets a passcode with Face ID to unlock. The passcode stays on that device.

Bank alerts

Which banks and countries does it work with?

It reads alert formats from banks and wallets in the Gulf, India and the rest of Asia, Africa, Europe, the US and Latin America, in English, Arabic, Spanish, Portuguese and many other languages, but not every format. Paste one of your own alerts into the check on the SMS Auto-Import screen before relying on it: it shows exactly what would be saved.

Bank SMS and email alerts
My bank only sends app notifications. Can TrackExpenses still log them?

Not automatically: no iPhone automation can read another app’s notification. iOS doesn’t let you copy a notification’s text, but if your bank’s app lets you copy a transaction’s details, tap Paste on the Transactions screen and the amount, shop and date are filled in, ready to check. Pasting is free and unlimited. Many banks can also send SMS or email alerts, which an automation can log.

Pasting an alert
Can it log bank alerts that arrive by email?

Yes. Add the email account, such as Gmail, to Apple’s Mail app, then create a Shortcuts Email automation filtered on a word from the alert subject, with the Import Bank Message action. Shortcuts can see Mail, not the Gmail app. The app shows the steps under More, SMS Auto-Import, Bank alerts by email.

Bank email alerts guide
What does the bank alert reader skip?

One-time codes, declined payments, fraud checks, payment requests, bills that are only due and promotions are left out. Anything uncertain, such as a possible duplicate or a card it doesn’t know yet, is saved and flagged for a look.

How many bank alerts are free?

Ten successful automatic imports per calendar month on each device, SMS and email together. Later alerts are caught and kept, locked outside your totals; Pro adds them all, otherwise next month’s allowance releases as many as it covers. Pasting an alert by hand is free and unlimited.

Free and Pro

Is TrackExpenses free?

Yes. The free ledger has unlimited manual entries, budgets with a marker for today, moving money between budgets, reports, search, budget alerts, bill reminders, multiple currencies, widgets and private iCloud sync, with 3 accounts and 2 recurring rules. Voice entry, automatic bank imports and AI questions to the coach are free 10 times a month on each device.

Free and Pro side by side
What does Pro add?

Unlimited voice entry, bank imports, accounts and recurring rules; coach challenges and many more coach questions; today’s daily budget, a daily amount for each budget and a daily budget for chosen categories; the salary plan, forecasts and what-if plans; Export and Share, CSV import and backup restore; themes and alternate app icons. Pro is a monthly or annual subscription or a one-time lifetime purchase, with prices shown in the app.

What happens when Pro or a free trial ends?

Nothing you saved is locked. Entries, accounts and repeating bills stay and keep working, even past the free limits. A Pro theme goes back to the standard one and a Pro app icon to the main icon, and both come back if you get Pro again. A short note says what changed.

Can I export my data?

Yes, with Pro. Export and Share, from the share button on Transactions or More, Backup and Export, writes a CSV of this month, the last 3 or 6 months, this fiscal year or all transactions, and the full JSON backup. Every export is part of Pro since version 1.15.0. Your records stay in the app either way.

Budgets, alerts and reminders

Can my budget month start on payday?

Yes. More, Period and Entry sets any start day from 1 to 31, and every list, chart, budget and total follows it. A day past the end of a short month means its last day. There is no rolling two-week period.

Payday budget guide
How is today’s budget worked out?

Your period’s budget, minus its repeating bills, paid or still to come, and what you spent before today, shared across the days left including today and rounded down. It holds still all day. The figure is part of Pro; when the budget is used up, the app says so for free.

How to set a daily spending limit
Can TrackExpenses warn me before I overspend?

Yes, for free. Turn on Budget alerts in More, Budgets: a notification when a budget passes 80% while ahead of its pace, and once when it goes over, with what is left and the days it has to last.

Get an alert before you overspend
Can it remind me about bills?

Yes, for free. Turn on Remind me the day before in More, Recurring: a notification at 9:00 the day before a repeating expense is due, with the amount and the account. Bills due on the same day come in one notification.

Get reminded before bills are due
Does it handle several currencies?

Yes, for free. Pick a main currency and log in more than 110 others at the rate your bank charged; every total and balance converts to the main currency. Rates are not fetched from the internet.

Money coach and voice

What does the money coach send to an AI?

Only open questions the phone can’t answer, and only after you agree on screen. It sends your question, the conversation so far and a summary of this month and last month, and, if the model asks, totals for up to 13 months or up to 20 purchases with date, shop, category and amount, through the developer’s relay to Anthropic’s Claude. It never sends notes, receipts, account names or numbers, or balances.

What leaves your phone
Which questions does the coach answer without AI?

What you spent today or yesterday, what is left in a category, your balances, how a named month went, whether you are on pace, where your money goes and how you compare with last month, plus charts, files and typed purchases. These are worked out on your phone and never use one of your questions.

Does voice entry send my audio anywhere?

No. Speech is recognized on your device, and every result is a draft you check before saving. Ten saved voice entries a month are free on each device; Pro makes it unlimited.

Languages and devices

Which languages is TrackExpenses in?

English, Arabic with a full right-to-left layout, German, Latin American Spanish and Brazilian Portuguese. Voice entry, the bank alert reader and the money coach work in all five. To change it, tap Language in More, which opens the app’s page in iOS Settings.

Does it work on iPad and Mac?

Yes. It runs on iPhone and iPad with iOS or iPadOS 17 or later, and on Mac computers with Apple silicon through the App Store. With iCloud sync on, all of them show the same ledger.

iCloud sync
Is there an Android version?

No, it is not on Google Play. TrackExpenses is on the App Store for iPhone and iPad, and runs on Mac computers with Apple silicon.

Can two people share one budget?

No. Sync joins the devices signed in to one Apple Account, and there are no shared budgets between two people.

Pro adds unlimited voice entry, SMS and email imports, accounts and recurring rules, money coach challenges and more coach questions, a salary plan, forecasts, overall and per-budget daily guidance with repeating bills accounted for first, a daily budget for chosen categories optionally up to a date, what-if planning, Export & Share (CSV of any range and the full JSON backup; every export is Pro since 1.15.0), CSV import, backup restore, themes and alternate icons. Monthly and annual subscriptions and a one-time lifetime purchase are available. Spending calculations depend on your records and are not financial advice.
