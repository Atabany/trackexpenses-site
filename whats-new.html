What’s new in TrackExpenses.

Every App Store release since 1.10.0, newest first, in plain words. The version on the App Store today is 1.16.0, released on 18 November 2026. Inside the app, More → What’s New lists the same releases.

By Mohamed Elatabany · Updated November 2026 · Suggest a correction

1.16.0 · 1.15.0 · 1.14.0 · 1.13.0 · 1.12.0 · 1.11.0 · 1.10.0

1.16.0 · November 2026

  • Bank alerts from Latin America: alerts from banks and wallets in Brazil, Mexico, Colombia, Argentina, Chile and Peru are read right, from Pix transfers to card purchases, whether you paste them or an automation brings them in. Verification codes and declined payments stay out of your ledger.
  • Now in Spanish and Portuguese: the whole app in Latin American Spanish and Brazilian Portuguese, voice entry and the money coach included.

1.15.0 · November 2026

  • Bank alerts from around the world: many more banks and wallets in the US, Europe, Asia, Africa, Latin America and the Gulf are read right; fraud checks, codes, payment requests and declined payments stay out.
  • Bank alerts by email: More → SMS Auto-Import shows how a Shortcuts automation logs bank emails too (guide).
  • Voice that hears you better: prices said the way you say them, lists without pauses, the spoken language picked on the listening screen, and one tap when “twelve fifty” is heard as 1250.
  • Pasted alerts land on the right account: the account linked to the card the message names, and the category you used before for that shop.
  • Quicker to type: arrows beside the date step a day, and a shop you’ve used before fills in its usual category and account.
  • Search adds it up: what you spent and what came in across everything it finds, in every period.
  • SMS setup in your language: Learn more opens the guide in Arabic or German, and setup suggests the word your bank’s alerts really contain.
  • When a trial ends: a short note says what changes; entries, accounts and repeating bills stay, and your theme and app icon come back with Pro.
  • Five new app icons: the coach in five new looks, from Starry Night to Sunset Shades. With Pro.
  • Export & Share: one screen for a spreadsheet of any month, any range or your whole ledger, and the full backup. With Pro, like every export from this version on.

1.14.0 · November 2026

  • Actions on the app icon: touch and hold the icon to say an expense, add one, paste a bank message or ask your coach.
  • A daily budget for what you choose: what you can spend per day from one category or a few, such as a trip, up to a date you pick. Your budgets stay as they are. With Pro (daily budget).
  • A clearer SMS setup walkthrough: the steps play inside the app, with sharp pictures, text that follows your text size and buttons to step back and forward.
  • A smaller download that takes less space on your iPhone.

1.13.0 · November 2026

  • Budget alerts: a notification when a budget passes 80% ahead of its pace, and when it goes over. Turn it on in Budget settings (alerts and reminders).
  • Bill reminders: a notification the morning before rent, a subscription or any repeating expense is due, with the amount and the account. Turn it on in More → Recurring.
  • A quicker coach: what you spent today, what is left for a category and what your accounts hold are answered on your phone, without using a question, and an answer can be stopped while it is written.
  • Currencies you say, read right: “dollars”, “pounds”, “rupees” or “riyals” mean your own money when your ledger is kept in it.
  • Scheduled payments: one that has gone through is logged as spending; one still to come is left alone.
  • Now in German: every screen, the widgets, Siri and voice entry.

1.12.0 · November 2026

  • Your money coach: ask about your money in your own words, type what you spent and it logs it on your phone, free, and it reads your month and suggests one limit. Ten AI questions a month free, more with Pro (money coach).
  • Getting started: a short checklist on your home screen that ticks itself as you go.
  • Siri, widgets and Control Center: ask Siri what you can spend today, log a coffee with one tap from a widget, keep your budget on the Lock Screen.
  • Extra income in your salary plan: a bonus or side income for one month only.
  • A calculator in your plan: type sums like 1200 + 350 into planned amounts, budgets and Move money.
  • Clearer amounts: the dirham reads AED, currency signs sit right against the figure, and chart axes use one tidy scale.
  • Smarter voice entry: “hotel 1200 for 2 nights”, “5 thousand rent”, “rent on November 1st” or just “Starbucks 22”, with the shop and category filled in for well-known brands.
  • Bank alerts read better: a purchase abroad imports at what your bank charged in your own currency, interest paid to you counts as income, a payment request is left alone, and a shop named in Arabic is read.

1.11.0 · November 2026

  • Settings that agree everywhere: if your devices end up with different months or currencies, More → iCloud Sync shows both and lets you keep one for all of them.
  • Dates in your region’s order: month first in the United States.

1.10.0 · November 2026

  • Your ledger on every device: everything you log syncs through your private iCloud to your iPhone, iPad and Mac, free (iCloud sync).
  • A plan for each month: each month in the salary plan can keep its own amounts, and a change carries to the months after. With Pro.
  • Notes on plan lines: a short note on any line of a month’s plan, so you remember why it is different.

Expense Tracker: SMS & Voice for iPhone, iPad and Mac.

5.0 on the App Store · 9 ratings across 6 countries · Checked 2026-11-20

App facts and free / Pro limits

Expense Tracker: SMS & Voice (TrackExpenses) is made by Mohamed Elatabany for iPhone and iPad with iOS/iPadOS 17 or later, and runs on Mac computers with Apple silicon. The app is in English, Arabic, German, Spanish and Brazilian Portuguese, with a full right-to-left Arabic interface. No app account or bank login is required. Optional Shortcuts automations import supported bank SMS, bank email alerts (through Apple’s Mail app) and Wallet purchases, and a bank SMS or email can be pasted; the app does not read your Messages inbox or your email. Voice entry creates on-device drafts on compatible devices and languages. The free ledger includes unlimited manual transactions, budgets, current-period budget moves and Cover with the total unchanged, days left and spent/left amounts, budget alerts (at 80% when ahead of pace, and when over), bill reminders the morning before a repeating expense is due, historical stats, search with Spent and Income totals across every period, private iCloud sync across iPhone, iPad and Mac, 3 accounts, 2 recurring rules, 10 saved voice entries per calendar month per device and 10 successful automatic bank SMS or email imports per calendar month per device. Touching and holding the app icon offers Say an expense, New expense, Paste bank message and Ask your coach. The money coach reads the last budget month and suggests one limit. Today’s spending, a category, account balances, any named month, charts, files and typed logging are worked out on the device without using a question; open questions go to Anthropic’s Claude through the developer’s relay only after the user agrees, with the conversation so far, a summary of this month and last month and, when the model asks, totals for up to 13 months or up to 20 purchases with their date, shop, category and amount; never notes, receipts, account names or numbers, or balances (10 a month free per device). SMS alerts past the free allowance are kept but locked outside totals; upgrading to Pro adds them all, otherwise the next calendar month’s allowance releases as many as it covers. Nothing is dropped and saved history is never locked; when Pro or a trial ends, entries, accounts and repeating bills keep working. Pro adds unlimited voice entry, SMS and email imports, accounts and recurring rules, money coach challenges and more coach questions, a salary plan, forecasts, overall and per-budget daily guidance with repeating bills accounted for first, a daily budget for chosen categories optionally up to a date, what-if planning, Export & Share (CSV of any range and the full JSON backup; every export is Pro since 1.15.0), CSV import, backup restore, themes and alternate icons. Monthly and annual subscriptions and a one-time lifetime purchase are available. Spending calculations depend on your records and are not financial advice.
